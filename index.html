Sydney, Australia

A country vibrant city known for its iconic landmarks like the Sydney Opera House and Sydney Harbour Bridge.

Melbourne, Australia

A cultural hub famous for its art, food, and diverse neighborhoods in the country known as downunder.

Tokyo, Japan

A bustling metropolis blending tradition and modernity, famous for its cherry blossoms and rich culture. Its a country worth seeing.

Kyoto, Japan

Known for its historic temples, gardens, and traditional tea houses. Don't miss out on this country.

Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

A lively city known for its stunning beaches, vibrant carnival celebrations, and iconic landmarks. This country is a mix of African, portuguese, and spanish culture.

São Paulo, Brazil

The financial hub with diverse culture, arts, and a vibrant nightlife. This is one of the reasons people choose to go on vacation to this country.

Angkor Wat, Cambodia

A UNESCO World Heritage site, the largest religious monument in the world, and this country is known for their temples.

Taj Mahal, India

An iconic symbol of love and a masterpiece of Mughal architecture. This country also known for Bollywood.

Amritsar, India

India is known for its vibrant festivals like Holi, the birthplace of major religions like Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, and Sikhism, its practice of yoga and Ayurveda. This country is known for its spices too.

Sihanoukville, Cambodia

Sihanoukville, Cambodia is known for its tropical beaches and crystal-clear waters. This country is known for its temples too.

Bora Bora, French Polynesia

An island known for its stunning turquoise waters, beaches, and luxurious overwater bungalows. This country is one of the best vacation spots.
